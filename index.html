Welcome to Jaunty

Every January, folks from a variety of communities form up to play some survival Minecraft. Gameplay is spiced up a tiny bit with the additon of proximity voice chat and a couple of plugins. Outside of that, it's kept pretty strictly vanilla. After the month has finished, things are wrapped up and folks go their own ways for the year. That's Jaunty. If you'd like to join us, feel free to click here for more information. You may also want to join the mailing list to receive updates.
